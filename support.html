Quick answers

The questions we get most. If yours is not here, just email us.

How do I reset my password?

Go to app.clarify.re/login and use the "Forgot password" link. If the reset email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then email us.

How do I update my account or billing details?

Sign in and open your account settings. For plan changes or invoice questions, email hello@clarify.re.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Email hello@clarify.re from the address on your account. You keep access through the end of your current billing period. No hoops, no retention calls.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email hello@clarify.re and we will delete your account and associated data. Our Privacy Policy explains what is retained and for how long.

How do I use Clarify.RE inside ChatGPT?

Open the Clarify.RE app in ChatGPT and sign in with your Clarify.RE credentials. The full walkthrough, including how to disconnect, is on the Clarify.RE for ChatGPT page.

Does Clarify.RE give legal or compliance advice?

No. Clarify.RE is software for organizing and managing transactions. It is not a brokerage, law firm, or regulator. Confirm legal and compliance questions with your broker or attorney.

What should I include in my email?

The email on your account, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and a screenshot if you have one. Please leave out client financial documents, Social Security numbers, and other sensitive data unless we ask for them through a secure channel.
