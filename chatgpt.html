Clarify.RE for ChatGPT

Last Updated: October 2026

Clarify.RE is available as an app inside ChatGPT, allowing you to look up transaction details, check deadlines, and review task status using natural language. This page explains how to connect, how your data is handled, and how to resolve common issues.

Need help? Email hello@clarify.re or visit our Support page.

1. Getting Started

  • You must have an active Clarify.RE account. If you do not have one yet, request early access.
  • In ChatGPT, open the Clarify.RE app and follow the prompt to sign in with your Clarify.RE credentials.
  • Once connected, you can ask ChatGPT questions about your transactions, such as "What deadlines are coming up this week?" or "What is the status of the inspection for 123 Main Street?"

2. Disconnecting the Integration

You can disconnect Clarify.RE from ChatGPT at any time:

  • In ChatGPT, open your settings, locate the Clarify.RE app, and remove or disconnect it.
  • To revoke access from the Clarify.RE side, or if you need help disconnecting, email hello@clarify.re and we will revoke the connection for you.

3. Data and Privacy in ChatGPT

When you use Clarify.RE through ChatGPT, only the information needed to answer your request is shared with the integration. We do not sell your data, and the same protections described in our Privacy Policy apply. Please note that OpenAI's own terms and privacy practices govern how ChatGPT handles your conversations.

4. Common ChatGPT Integration Issues

  • "I can't sign in." Confirm you can sign in at app.clarify.re first. If that works, try disconnecting and reconnecting the app in ChatGPT.
  • "ChatGPT says it can't find my transaction." Make sure the transaction exists in your Clarify.RE account and try referring to it by its full property address.
  • "The information looks out of date." Ask ChatGPT to check again. If the problem persists, sign out of the integration and sign back in.
  • Anything else: Email us with a description of what you asked and the response you received.
